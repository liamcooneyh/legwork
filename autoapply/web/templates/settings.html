How applying works

Each run finds and scores new jobs, then prepares a draft for the good fits: a tailored resume, a cover letter if the form asks for one, and an answer to every question. Nothing is sent. Open a draft with Fill in Chrome: the extension fills the form in your own Chrome and you press Submit.
